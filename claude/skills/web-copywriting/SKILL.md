## Activation Contract

Use for writing or revising website copy: commercial landing, product, comparison, category, service pages, headings, CTAs, and SEO copy. Support any business, locale, and tone. Exclude backend work, legal advice, and unsupported psychology claims.

## Hard Rules

- Establish audience, locale, tone, intent, objective, offer, and known repo/brief facts before writing. Ask only for missing decisions; never repeat answered gates.
- Build `evidence -> feature -> benefit -> use case -> honest differentiation`. Treat sourced features as untested claims. Qualify or omit unknown material claims. Never invent urgency, scarcity, ratings, savings, firsthand experience, health guarantees, approvals, ownership, sources, licences, or terms.
- Lead with buyer benefits and decision help before a giant hero/process story. Recommend when supported, naming audience and comparison. Do not claim universal superiority; compare models on identical criteria.
- Write concise, natural, positive copy. Hide internal audit boilerplate and phrases such as “evidencia visual”, “medidas atribuidas”, “kit observado”, or “punto de vigilancia”; use “Ver análisis”, “Qué incluye”, and “Ten en cuenta”. Keep material, affiliate, and safety disclosures nearby.
- Verify every link's real destination; never label a document/source link “Comprar”. Match product photos to exact variant/kit and write factual alt text.
- Map existing keyword intent and canonical URL. Use the primary term naturally in a descriptive title/H1 and relevant H2; use synonyms naturally. Distinguish home hub, best-list category, and exact-product intent. No density quotas, title-length hacks, fake freshness/year claims, ranking guarantees, mandatory word counts, or filler; lengths are editorial estimates only when requested.

## Decision Gates

| Gate | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Fact supported? | State it, qualify it, or omit it. |
| Page/link role clear? | Match intent and destination before drafting. |
| Caveat material? | Put it next to the claim or CTA. |

## Execution Steps

1. Gather facts, audience, intent, objections, safety constraints, and URL map.
2. Draft benefit-first copy, objection handling, and clear next step.
3. Load installed `seo`, `laws-of-ux`, or `impeccable` guidance when relevant; do not require absent skills or hardcode paths.
4. Read aloud. Check exact benefit, readability, next step, objections, safety, evidence, coherence of headings/content/images/alt/links, and comparison criteria. Keep verification notes internal, not in visitor CTAs.

## Output Contract

Return final ready-to-use copy first. Add only unresolved factual approvals or brief rationale when asked. Change only authorized copy scope; do not publish or modify production app, templates, content, database, or design merely to pass this check.
